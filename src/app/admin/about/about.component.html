Student Clearance and Monitoring System

Purpose

This Student Clearance and Monitoring System is designed to streamline and automate the process of tracking and managing student clearances. It provides an efficient platform for administrators to monitor student requirements and for students to track their clearance status in real-time.

How It Works

📝

Step 1: Requirements Posted

Admin creates and posts clearance requirements for specific semester and academic year

↓
📋

Step 2: Auto-Assignment

System automatically assigns requirements to all students in that semester

↓
👨‍🎓

Step 3: Student Compliance

Students view their pending requirements and comply necessary documents

↓
✅

Step 4: Admin Approval

Admin reviews submissions and approves completed requirements

↓
🎓

Step 5: Clearance Complete

Once all requirements are approved, student receives complete clearance status
